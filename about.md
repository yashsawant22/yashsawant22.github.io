      <h1>I’m Yash.</h1>
      <p>I build and study language models. At Wethos AI, I work on post-training, personalization, and models with persistent memory.</p>
    </div>
  </header>

  <div class="about-prose">
    <p>Most of my independent work starts with a model doing something its training objective or evaluation does not fully explain. I’ve looked at <a href="{{ '/2026/05/09/adaptive-lora-sft-vs-grpo.html' | relative_url }}">why a rank-allocation method that works under SFT fails under GRPO</a>, whether <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.26460">personalization metrics measure the person or only surface resemblance</a>, and <a href="{{ '/2026/08/02/rl-teaches-when-not-how.html' | relative_url }}">what one RL update changes inside a model</a>.</p>

    <p>I tend to understand these questions by building the system, finding where the abstraction breaks, and testing the explanation directly. That is also why I <a href="{{ '/writing/' | relative_url }}">build models from scratch and write about experiments that fail</a>: both are useful ways of finding out which parts I don’t actually understand.</p>

    <p>I came to this through applied machine learning. Before Wethos, I built language-model systems at BNY Mellon and JerseySTEM. I studied data science at UC Irvine and NMIMS University.</p>
